# Gene panel annotation record

The 12 Figure 6 groups contain 67 genes: 41 genes selected from the broader 108-gene candidate panel and 26 additional immune-interaction genes. The broader panel contains 117 gene-chain assignments. These are distinct annotation objects.

The JSON metadata accompanying the broader panel were corrected on 20 September 2026. The original recorded timestamp and the SHA-256 of the supplied historical JSON are retained. Gene membership, chain assignments and the recorded SHA-256 of the panel TSV were not changed. The corrected JSON is not presented as an unchanged historical freeze or as evidence of prospective registration.

GO:0061702 denotes the canonical inflammasome complex. It is not specific to NLRP3. The remaining term labels were reconciled with the official QuickGO labels recorded in the metadata. The original ontology query response is not included, so the original extraction procedure cannot be independently reconstructed from this package.

Historical analysis rules concerning prospective registration, TCGA coverage and a silencing classifier do not describe the analysis in Figure 6 and have been removed from the current descriptive metadata. The broader panel does not define a measured PANoptosis activity score. Scientific labels and functional qualifications for the plotted groups are provided in GROUP_ANNOTATIONS.tsv.

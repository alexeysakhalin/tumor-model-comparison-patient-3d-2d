# Figure 6

**Expression representation and functional effects of selected cell-death and immune-interaction gene sets across epithelial tumor models.**

**a,** Twelve gene sets are compared across two expression contrasts, seven CRISPR selection arms and a published candidate list. Colors of the row labels identify organizational domains; the four headings of panel c abbreviate them as Receptors (death-receptor signalling), Inhibition (cell-death inhibition), Death machinery (regulated cell-death machinery) and Recognition (immune recognition and modulation). Full membership and scientific labels are provided in tables/IKM_gene_membership_by_group.tsv. Each of the 67 genes contributes to one plotted group. The domains do not represent independent sequential barriers to immune killing.

The expression contrasts compare malignant with healthy epithelium and 3D dome cultures with 2D adherent models within six anatomical sites. The cohorts comprise 20 healthy epithelial profiles from 11 donors, 231 malignant epithelial profiles from 202 study-qualified patients in 34 studies, 152 dome models and 401 adherent models. The sites are stomach, large intestine, lung, breast, pancreas and ovary. These are separate cohorts matched by anatomical site. Healthy epithelial data derive from Tabula Sapiens 2.0, malignant epithelial data from Kang et al., and cultured-model data from DepMap and the NextGen collection. Outside the reference band: Death-inhibitory module, Death-receptor mediators for malignant minus healthy, and MHC-I antigen processing and presentation for organoid minus 2D.

The functional block contains six arms from Watterson et al. and one pooled HeLa arm from Zhou et al., covering five models and 19 screen contrasts. Each arm combines a model and a selection condition: IFN-gamma in SK-MEL-2, A375, HT-29, HT-29 JAK1 knockout and CRC-9; autologous T cells in CRC-9; and TNF-alpha plus IFN-gamma in HeLa. Column headings identify models and the row below the matrix identifies selection conditions. The two CRC-9 columns represent the same tumoroid model under different conditions. HeLa library halves form one screen and are not biological replicates. Values are group-median effects relative to the genomic median, divided by half the width of the arm-specific 95% reference band. Positive values indicate relative knockout enrichment under selection; negative values indicate depletion. The latter may reflect sensitization or reduced relative fitness. Screen counts and replicate Spearman correlations are shown below the matrix; a dash indicates that no biological replicate estimate is available.

Both continuous blocks use reference-band units. Solid frames mark values outside their reference band with a consistent sign across contributing organs or screens; dotted frames mark values outside the band with sign disagreement. Because a band can be asymmetric about zero, a normalized magnitude above one is not necessarily outside it. Frames are descriptive and do not denote multiplicity-adjusted significance.

The right-hand column reports overlap with the published Mel624 NY-ESO-1 TCR T-cell selection list of Patel et al. Fractions give the number of group genes in the list divided by group size; the green scale represents that fraction. The comparison uses 502 non-miRNA identifiers from the 554-identifier published list. A zero denotes no overlap with that list, not an experimental negative. No new enrichment test is reported.

**b,** Published HT-29 siRNA viability-rescue Z scores from Woznicki et al. are plotted against percentiles in the HT-29 CRISPR ranking from Watterson et al. All 34 published protective candidates are matched after identifier reconciliation. Filled points identify JAK1 and JAK2, which met the original siRNA study's high-confidence criterion. The shaded region marks the top CRISPR decile and the horizontal line marks the median. The siRNA screen used TNF-alpha plus IFN-gamma; the CRISPR screen used IFN-gamma alone. The comparison therefore involves different perturbations, libraries and stimuli.

**c,** Upper plot: group expression scores relative to matched control genes. Within-profile percentile ranks are calculated over 18,612 shared genes. Each target gene is compared with 400 genes matched by expression rank in the 2D reference of the same anatomical site. Scores are aggregated across genes within a profile, profiles within a site and the six sites. Intervals are 95% conditional cluster-bootstrap intervals from 2,000 accepted replicates, resampling donors, studies or model identifiers as appropriate. Cluster draws are applied jointly across sites, and draws omitting a site are rejected. Gastric malignant samples derive from one study and cannot estimate between-study variability for that stratum. Filled markers indicate intervals excluding zero; hollow markers indicate intervals containing zero. The 2D reference is approximately centered on zero by construction. Lower plot: median across the seven arms of the absolute normalized group statistic. Fractions indicate the number of arms outside their reference band, including both concordant and discordant frames.

The gene sets describe selected molecular components. RNA scores do not establish caspase cleavage, MLKL phosphorylation, gasdermin activation or a particular death modality. MLKL/PGAM5 is a descriptive pair because PGAM5 is dispensable for necroptosis in tested systems; a separate sensitivity analysis finds MLKL alone inside its one-gene reference band in all four layers. PGAM5 is outside its band in malignant epithelium under both reconstructed Monte Carlo sampling and exact enumeration of the 200-gene reference pool. Its healthy-layer classification depends on which of those methods is used. These auxiliary results do not change the plotted pair or establish necroptotic competence; details are in PGAM5_SENSITIVITY_METHODS.md. NK receptor and immune-regulatory ligands have receptor-dependent effects. IFN receptor and JAK-STAT genes are not a separate expression group. Primary-source citations and data components are listed in SOURCE_DATASETS.tsv.
